# OptiTalent as a Workforce Operating System

Thesis: do not ship another HRMS with more modules. Ship an OS that understands what is happening and helps HR, managers, and employees decide what to do next.

## Layers

1. **System of record** — employees, payroll, attendance, leave, ATS, performance (dataquery).
2. **Workforce graph** — skills, journey events, teams, goals, compensation bands.
3. **Intelligence** — digital twin, attrition attention, why engine, work health, people brief.
4. **Agents** — Chief of Staff executes onboarding, approvals, recipes.
5. **Simulation** — hire N, attrition %, bottlenecks.

## What we will not USP

Attendance, payroll, leave, a generic chatbot, a dashboard, recruitment, or a mobile shell — those are table stakes already covered by Workday, SAP, Oracle, Darwinbox, Keka, greytHR, Zoho People, BambooHR, Rippling, ADP, UKG.

## Decision support

Risk percentages are not automated judgments about a person. Copy on twin, copilot, and command center states this explicitly.
